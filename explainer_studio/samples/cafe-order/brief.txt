Title: How a café gets your coffee to you

Who it's for: anyone, no background needed.

What they should come away knowing:
- An order becomes a ticket on the kitchen screen and joins a queue.
- Tickets are made first in, first out, so yours waits for the ones ahead.
- One drink takes about a minute. The espresso and the milk are done at the
  same time, which saves about twenty seconds.
- Your name is called, you pick it up, and many systems work the same way:
  requests wait in a queue and are served in turn.

Length: about a minute and a half, three scenes.
Tone: friendly and plain. Say that the times are typical estimates.
