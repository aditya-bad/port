# Learnings: the monthly strangle (strangle_monthly_v2)

My notes for future reference. These are probable learnings, not for
sure.

## 1. Don't start a new cycle on the day an intraday cycle exits

*1 Nov 2026*

If a cycle is entered today and exited today, don't take the new cycle's
positions the same day. Take them the next day. If the cycle ran more
than 2 days, entering the next one the same day is fine.

Example: cycle 16 (I haven't looked at the cycles before it). The cycle
before it was entered and exited on the same day, with a profit of about
₹750. The next cycle, cycle 16, was entered that same day, and it went
terribly wrong: the only trade that has.
