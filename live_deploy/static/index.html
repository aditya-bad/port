Clear all deployments

Permanently deletes every deployment — positions, trades, events, and equity snapshots included. Cannot be undone. Your Kite login, subscribed instruments, and Admin Options settings are not affected.

Redeploy latest version

Pulls the latest code, rebuilds, and restarts the whole app on the server. Every deployment's own state is safe (it lives in Postgres, not in this process) — but the app itself will go unreachable for roughly 1–3 minutes while it rebuilds, then come back on its own. This page will detect that and reload automatically.
