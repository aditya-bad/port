02 Colour
Every colour is a CSS variable on the root, so themes swap values without touching components. Green and red mean profit and loss only. Yellow means attention only. Buttons use ink, never green or red.
--{{ c.k2 }}{{ c.u }}
Themes
Paper (default, warm off-white), Slate (cool daylight), Night (warm charcoal). Night lifts green, red and orange in lightness so contrast holds at 4.5:1 on charcoal. Attention yellow becomes a deep mustard ground with light text.
Series colours
Ink, orange, blue, teal, plum, in that order, for Compare and multi-series charts. Never green or red, which are reserved for signed values.
Signed tints
--gs and --rs are 12% tints of gain and loss for area fills, heat cells and drawdown. Heat cells scale opacity 12% to 67% by magnitude.
03 Type
Three families, one job each. Funnel Display for headlines and hero numbers. Funnel Sans for everything you read. Martian Mono for every figure that sits in a column or ticks, so digits never shift.

Primary = filled ink, one per area. Secondary = 1.5px ink outline. Tertiary = line outline. Disabled = soft fill and mute text, and the reason is always written nearby. A trailing ellipsis means a confirmation follows. Square corners everywhere.

Cells mix --pos or --r into paper from 16% to 90% by size. Text flips to paper above 60%. Year cell is outlined in ink; Running total and Avg row follow. Toggle % of capital or ₹.
Trading calendar
Weekdays only, Mon–Fri rows, weeks as columns. Four steps each way, scaled to the 90th-percentile day so one outlier doesn't wash out the rest. Soft = flat day, outline = not running. Range: last 12 months or a year.

One card for every chart: bold title, then label and value rows. Line charts add a vertical ink guide and a ringed dot per series. Desktop follows the pointer; phone sits above the finger and scrubs on sideways drag.
Cycle journey bar
All rows share one scale. Red tint from ₹0 to the worst point while open, solid bar from ₹0 to the close. Read across rows to see which cycles were saved late and which never went red.
